# Code of Conduct

## Our standards

We are committed to a welcoming, respectful, and harassment-free community. Constructive
participation includes using inclusive language, accepting feedback, focusing on what is best for
the project, and showing empathy toward other contributors.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized attention,
publishing another person's private information, or sustained disruption of project work.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately through the repository's maintainers or GitHub moderation tools.
Maintainers may remove or reject comments, commits, issues, and other contributions that violate
these standards, and may temporarily or permanently restrict participation.

This policy applies in project spaces and when someone represents the project in public.
